A state picture has no frame line but lies on a faint rounded translucent panel, a little lighter than the background on a dark terminal and a little darker on a light one (from `--background`), so it differs from a move icon. The start picture is one and a half times as large as an icon, on rows of its own. The output ends with `End state: TOKEN`, the end picture at the start size, a blank line and `Moves:` with the plain move string. This replaces the thin frame named in the earlier wording note.
