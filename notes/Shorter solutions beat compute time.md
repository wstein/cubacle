The owner measures the product by how many moves a human still has to make. Fewer moves always win over compute time or display niceties; a change that lengthens delivered solutions needs measured numbers shown to the owner first, and reporting fixes (stages, phases) are preferred over changes to the solution. Every optional shortcut therefore returns its answer only when it is shorter than the plain one.
