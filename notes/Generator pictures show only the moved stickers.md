Planned: `patterns show --icons` gets a mode in which unmoved stickers are drawn as `.` or `?`, so only what the generator changes is visible; `patterns find` gets the same. The reason is readability: on a big cube a generator moves few stickers and the full picture hides them. Defaults stay byte-identical. Don't-care targets for `--to` such as cross and f2l come later, after a concept paper.
