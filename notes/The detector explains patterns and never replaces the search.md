Symmetry is a hint, not a proof: the owner's own cube has no symmetry yet is a product of four macros, and a random cube costs only one to two seconds of pattern search. The detector therefore orders the search and explains the result (verbose output of solve names the family and the symmetry), and it never lets solve skip the pattern search, because a skipped search could cost the owner moves. Order of work: symmetry, canonical form with the library, orbit fingerprint and subgroup tests first, then layer projection and decomposition, the measure and the verbose hint.
