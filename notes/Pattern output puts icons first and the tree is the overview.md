Owner decision of 2026-10-25: for patterns the picture comes first and the text second, and the overview is a tree by category. `patterns --tree` exists: per pattern its catalog id, name, number of generators and shortest length per size, also with `--sort` and `--min-patternness`; the flat list stays the default. The pattern icons are in the program now: `patterns --icons` draws the list, `show`, `find` and `identify`.
