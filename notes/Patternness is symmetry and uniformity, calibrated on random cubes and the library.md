Patternness is `100 * (0.6 S + 0.4 U)`: S is the symmetry (any symmetry beyond the identity gives 1, colour-keeping operations count extra) and U the regularity of the piece orbits (simple shares of displaced and flipped pieces, one cycle length). Random cubes of 3x3x3 to 7x7x7 stay at 40 or below (1000 each), the solved cube and the library patterns are 100. A first version that needed six symmetries for full marks put only 46 % of an external pattern table at 95 or more, because many known patterns have two to four symmetries; the saturation moved to one symmetry and the weights to 0.6 and 0.4. The word is patternness or similarity, never probability.
