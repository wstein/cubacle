An Orbit64 token cannot prove that its producer used the same slot numbering as the draft convention (standard Kociemba corner order URF UFL ULB UBR DFR DLF DBL DRB and edge order UR UF UL UB DR DF DL DB FR FL BL BR; wings and centres by facelet index). The decoder therefore has to trust the producer.
