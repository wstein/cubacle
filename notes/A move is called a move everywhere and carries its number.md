The owner asked for one word for one turn: move, as in at-move and the move count, with step kept for engine stages only. Each move icon shows its number in the corner, counted over the whole solution so it is the argument of the at-move option of recover, and the phase line carries the range of its moves without verbose mode. State pictures have no headline and a thin frame, a state picture takes the space of two move icons in the row after its phase, and the start picture has rows of its own, one and a half times as large, above the first phase. The folded view's gap to the flat faces was halved at the same time.
