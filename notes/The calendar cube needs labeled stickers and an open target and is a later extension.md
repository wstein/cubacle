The calendar cube (a 3x3x3 whose stickers carry weekday, month and day instead of colors; the goal is to show a date, not to be solved) was looked at on 2026-10-25 as an idea only; the textures and layout on the author's page stay his and nothing is taken from them. It asks for two things cubacle lacks. First, an open target: any state that shows the date is a goal, which is the don't-care target that exists today for the 2x2x2 and 3x3x3 and is still to be widened to the big cubes. Second, labeled stickers: 54 distinct marks with a turn, so that equal marks are no longer interchangeable, a deliberate exception to the color-based model for that one input type. The owner ordered the work as open targets on the big cubes first, then labeled stickers, then a `calendar` command with an own layout, then marks as picture output, each after the pattern-aware solving and the stable relative state.
