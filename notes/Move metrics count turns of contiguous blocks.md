The seven metrics (HTM, QTM, STM, ETM, OBTM, BTM, ATM) are counted on one unit: a turn of contiguous layers about one axis, a rotation being the block of every layer. HTM and OBTM are one rule (outer-touching block 1, inner block 2), STM counts the layers moved, rotations are free except in ETM. Where sources differ the tool takes the choice that never undercounts, and counts move words as typed so that `M`, `x` and `2-3Rw` are single turns, not the several layer moves the expression reader lowers them to.
